Overview

Awadh Aero Adventures Association is a registered non-profit organization dedicated to revolutionizing India's aerial adventure and aviation landscape. Based in Mankapur, Uttar Pradesh, the Association offers certified training programs in high-flying pursuits—from paragliding and power hang gliding to microlight flying—alongside cutting-edge drone technology and unmanned aviation training. With a commitment to safety, skill development, and structured ecosystem building, Awadh Aero combines exhilarating recreational flying experiences with professional aeronautical education. Through workshops, awareness camps, and strategic collaborations with government and industry partners, the Association empowers youth, adventure enthusiasts, and aspiring aviators while fostering regional tourism and employment opportunities in the aviation sector.

About
Awadh Aero DAC Aviation Academy

aadaa is a next-generation, state-of-the-art Aircraft Maintenance Engineering (AME) training institute built to support India's rapidly growing aviation industry. The academy focuses on developing highly skilled aviation professionals through industry-oriented training, digital transformation, and AI-powered learning methodologies. Its objective is to bridge the growing gap between India's aviation industry's demand for qualified aircraft engineers, technicians, and aviation support professionals by providing practical, hands-on training aligned with current industry standards.

AADAA CAMPUS INFRASTRUCTURE

Hangar

The institute features a wellequipped aircraft hangar for practical training, giving students hands-on experience with aircraft systems, tools, and maintenance procedures in a real aviation environment.

Workshops

Advanced workshops provide practical exposure to aircraft components, tools, and maintenance practices. These workshops help students strengthen their technical and hands-on engineering skills.

Computer Lab

The modern computer lab features aviation-related software for technical training and digital learning, allowing students to develop the practical computer and analytical skills required in the aviation industry.

Smart Classrooms

Modern class rooms with advanced teaching aids create an interactive and professional learning environment. The classrooms are designed to support both theoretical and technical aviation studies effectively.

Library

The library offers an extensive collection of aviation, engineering, and academic books, technical journals, and study materials. It provides a quiet, resourceful environment for learning, research, and self-study, with 24/7 access available for students.

Campus Wi-Fi

High-speed Wi-Fi across campus ensures students have convenient access to digital resources, aviation materials, and online learning platforms for academic and technical studies.

MESS FACILITY

The mess offers hygienic, nutritious meals in a comfortable dining environment, with proper attention to cleanliness, quality, and student convenience.

Hostel Facilities

The hostel facility provides a safe, comfortable, and disciplined living environment for students. Well-maintained rooms with essential amenities are designed to ensure a convenient and student-friendly stay within the campus environment. The hostel also offers proper security, hygiene, Wi-Fi connectivity, and recreational facilities to support both academic focus and overall well-being of students.

Extra-Curricular Activities

PERSONALITY DEVELOPMENT PROGRAMS

This program helps students build confidence, communication, and leadership skills through interactive sessions, grooming, and practical exposure. By developing strong interpersonal qualities and professional discipline, students become career-ready individuals prepared for both personal and professional success.

DRONE FLYING & MAINTENANCE TRAINING

AADAA Academy offers hands-on training in Drone Flying & Maintenance using small-class training drones. Students learn flying techniques, operational safety, and basic maintenance through practical sessions and live demonstrations—building industry-ready skills for the growing drone and aviation sector.

ENGLISH SPEAKING CLASSES

These programs at AADAA focus on improving the communication skills, technical vocabulary, and professional interaction needed for the aviation industry. Through practical speaking, presentations, and targeted training, students build the English fluency and confidence required to excel in interviews, teamwork, and aviation environments.

TRAINING ON AI-POWERED AIRCRAFT MAINTENANCE SOFTWARE

Students will train on the M-DAC System, an AI-powered software used in modern aircraft maintenance. This training covers maintenance planning, technical record management, inventory monitoring, and data-driven decision-making.

GUEST LECTURES BY INDUSTRY EXPERTS

Experienced professionals share practical knowledge, industry trends, and real-world insights to help students understand professional aviation environments. Through direct interaction with these experts, students enhance their technical understanding, industry awareness, and overall career readiness.
