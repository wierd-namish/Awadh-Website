Power Hang Glider Training

Power hang gliding (or weight-shift control trikes) combines the freedom of a standard hang glider wing with the reliability of a small fuel-efficient engine. It is the perfect entry point for flight enthusiasts.

Our curriculum begins with wing assembly and check routines, progressing through engine maintenance, wind gust vectors, emergency glides, and hands-on flying hours with certified instructors.

Syllabus
Phase 1: Rigging & Aerodynamics
  • Wing Batten:
    Insertions & rigging procedure

    Inspection & condition assessment
  • Weight-Shift:
    Control limits & balance

    Engine spark checks
Phase 2: Runway Takeoffs
  • Throttle Control:
    Power management on runway
  • Alignment Checks:
    Pre-takeoff alignment procedure
  • Climb Rate:
    Angle & rate of climb
  • Wind Drift:
    Corrections during takeoff
  • Circuit Patterns:
    Flying standard circuit patterns
Phase 3: Cross Country & Glides
  • Emergency Glide:
    Engine-out glide profiles
  • Wind Thermal Maps:
    Reading & interpretation
  • Precision Landings:
    Runway touchdown accuracy
